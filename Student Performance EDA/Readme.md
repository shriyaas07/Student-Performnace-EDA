# Student Performance EDA Project

This project explores the academic performance of students using exploratory data analysis (EDA). The analysis is based on a dataset of Portuguese secondary school students and aims to uncover relationships between factors like study time, internet access, prior grades, and final performance.

---

## 📁 Dataset Information

- **Source**: Kaagle  
- **File**: `student-por.csv`  
- **Description**: Data includes student demographic details, lifestyle habits, and academic performance (G1, G2, G3).

---

## 🔍 Objectives

- Understand key variables affecting student performance.
- Identify trends and relationships using visual analysis.
- Derive actionable insights based on the data.

---

## 📊 Tools Used

- **Python** (Jupyter Notebook)
- **Libraries**: Pandas,Numpy,Matplotlib,Seaborn

---

## 📈 Key Visualizations & Insights

### 1. Gender Distribution
- Majority of students are **female**, indicating a gender imbalance.

### 2. Age Distribution
- Most students fall in the **15–18 years** age range.
- **16 years** is the most frequent age.

### 3. Study Time vs Final Grades
- Students who study more tend to perform better in final grades.

### 4. G1 vs G3 (Scatter Plot with Trendline)
- There is a **strong positive correlation** between the first period grade (G1) and the final grade (G3).
- Indicates performance consistency across terms.

### 5. Internet Access vs Final Grades
- Students with **internet access at home** tend to have **slightly better academic performance**.

### 6. Correlation Matrix
- **Final grade (G3)** is highly correlated with **G1** and **G2**.
- **Study time** shows a mild positive correlation; **failures** and **absences** show negative correlations.
